Legacy CMS was limited, hard to maintain, and approaching deprecation

Ordering Experience case
Use this summary:

Ordering flow had major friction, especially in location selection
Problem: conversion was only 6%
Solution: bypassed the friction point with a more direct flow and added loyalty touchpoints strategically
Outcomes: conversion increased to 8%, loyalty adoption improved, integrations became possible, performance and ADA compliance improved
